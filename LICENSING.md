uses the proprietary **All Rights Reserved** terms in the root
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

Studio and designated copyright identity: **Grey Door Republik**.
Creator/developer credit: **WeeJubya**.

The licence permits recipients of an authorised Beta.2 package to install
and play it for beta testing. It does not generally authorise redistribution
or reuse of the game source or executables.

The root `LICENSE` is copied into the Windows Release output next to
`Floppy144.exe`. CI confirms that the packaged copy matches exactly.

**This does not mean Beta.2 has been publicly released or approved.**
Desktop visual and audio acceptance, authentic Beta.1 save migration,
final release package/rights review and explicit publication authorisation
remain separate gates in `docs/beta2/STATUS.md`.

## Public Beta.1 and historical permissions

Public Floppy//144 Beta.1 remains GPLv3-licensed. All rights previously
granted to its recipients remain in force for their Beta.1 copies.

The **original GPLv3 licence text and River2D/BadAcronym notice** are
preserved *byte-for-byte* at
[`licenses/history/Beta1-GPL-3.0.txt`](licenses/history/Beta1-GPL-3.0.txt).
The original developer Git history and published Beta.1 release/tag have
not been rewritten.

The public `GreyDoorRepublik/Floppy144-beta` repository also has its own
GPLv3 `LICENSE`, added as a separate Beta.1 historical correction.
This new Beta.2 licence does not apply to that release.
