# BF22: New procedural music scenes

**PLEASE HOLD**: menu theme, 70 BPM in A-minor-adjacent melodic fragments; restrained electric piano, sustained pad, celesta and sparse mechanical woodblock. 16-bar seamless instrumental cycle.

**YOUR CONTRIBUTION HAS BEEN NOTED**: 65 BPM, reflective acoustic piano with soft strings, vibraphone and occasional pitch-adjacent dissonance. Independent 16-bar cycle.

Both scenes are generated by code on the existing WinMM MIDI output, with no stored MIDI/audio. The original gameplay `process_step` remains unchanged: 80 BPM, deterministic per-Act corruption and the approved original composition. Scene changes send All Notes Off, restore proper GM instruments and restart only the new scene; ordinary Act updates do not restart the gameplay melody. Music volume/mute is shared.

Menu, Profile, Settings and standard Credits route to PLEASE HOLD. Completion and its Credits route to the completion theme. Other active screens use unchanged gameplay music. Splash and the Grey Door remain unaffected.

Listening tool: `tools/build_audio_review.ps1` builds `f144_audio_review.exe`. During listening, press **M** for menu, **C** for completion and **G** for the approved gameplay track, with UP/DOWN adjusting Music volume. These new compositions remain subject to the developer's approval; existing approved gameplay music is preserved.

Automated acceptance: Stage 4 audio contract (scene changes, original Act continuity) and `tools/test_bugfix22_music.ps1`; native review on an actual MIDI device is required.
