# FLOPPY//144

**A forgotten floppy disk. A government department. 1,440 KB of unanswered questions.**

*A retro-inspired exploration and mystery game from Grey Door Republik.*

---

## Welcome to the Government Department of Records

You have found an old floppy disk.

Naturally, you decide to see what's on it.

What follows is an unexpected connection to the **Government Department of Records (GDR)**, an institution apparently dedicated to the preservation, organisation and occasional misplacement of information.

The department's records are fragmented. Its offices are strangely quiet. Its computer systems appear to have survived considerably longer than anyone intended.

Your assignment is straightforward:

**Recover the records. Reconstruct the department. Establish what happened.**

The GDR would like to remind you that curiosity is not an officially recognised job requirement.

It may, however, prove useful.

## About the Game

**Floppy//144** is a single-player, retro-styled exploration and puzzle game combining office exploration, computer terminal interaction, document recovery and environmental storytelling.

Inspired by the computing experience of an earlier era, it features a deliberately restrained visual style, procedural sound and music, and the peculiar atmosphere of a government office that has been left to its own devices.

### Features

- **Explore the department:** Navigate offices, corridors and departmental facilities, inspecting furniture, equipment and anything that looks remotely important.
- **Recover lost information:** Operate an old-fashioned command-line terminal to restore damaged collections of records.
- **Investigate the paperwork:** Read recovered documents, discover connections and gradually piece together the department's history.
- **Manage limited storage:** With just 1,440 KB of recovery capacity, not everything can be recovered at once.
- **Keep track of discoveries:** Your Recovery Notebook records important findings and documents.
- **Choose your approach:** Multiple difficulty settings offer different levels of assistance.
- **Experience a changing environment:** Some details may differ between sessions.
- **Enjoy a retro presentation:** Pixel graphics, CRT effects, terminal interfaces and procedurally generated audio.

There is no requirement to have worked in the Civil Service.

Experience with unnecessarily complicated administrative procedures may be advantageous.

## Controls

Floppy//144 is primarily keyboard-controlled.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move around the department or navigate menus |
| `A` | Access nearby terminals and other accessible objects |
| `I` | Inspect nearby objects |
| `N` | Open or close the Recovery Notebook |
| `Enter` | Confirm a selection or action |
| `Backspace` | Go back where supported |
| `Page Up / Page Down` | Scroll through Notebook content |
| `Esc` | Open GDR Session Control |

### Using the Terminal

The archive terminal accepts typed commands.

Type `HELP` at the terminal prompt for operating instructions and available commands.

Press `Enter` to submit a command. The up and down arrow keys can recall previously entered commands.

Additional instructions are provided within the game.

**A small piece of advice:** Read things carefully. The department has an unfortunate habit of putting important information in places where nobody would reasonably expect to find it.

## Saving Your Progress

Floppy//144 supports manual saving, loading and automatic saving.

Press `Esc` to open **GDR Session Control**, where you can access the available session options.

Autosave frequency and other preferences can be adjusted in Settings.

We recommend saving regularly, particularly while playing a beta version.

## Beta Testing

**Floppy//144 is currently in beta testing.**

The game is playable, but you may encounter bugs, unexpected behaviour, presentation issues or aspects of the experience that need further refinement.

Your feedback will help improve the game before its full release.

### What We're Interested In

We're particularly interested in hearing about:

- Bugs, crashes or unexpected behaviour.
- Problems with movement, interactions or object inspection.
- Terminal commands that do not behave as expected.
- Confusing instructions, puzzles or objectives.
- Issues with saving and loading.
- Graphics, text, sound or music problems.
- Anything that makes the game frustrating for the wrong reasons.
- Anything you particularly enjoyed.

If something feels wrong, confusing or oddly suspicious, we'd like to hear about it.

Unless it's part of the mystery, of course.

### Reporting a Bug

Please use the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/GreyDoorRepublik/Floppy144-beta/issues) to report problems or suggest improvements.

Where possible, include:

1. **What happened:** A brief description of the problem.
2. **What you expected:** What you thought should happen instead.
3. **How to reproduce it:** The steps that led to the issue.
4. **Game version:** The beta version you were playing.
5. **Windows version:** Your operating system.
6. **Supporting evidence:** Screenshots or a short video, if useful.

For crash or save-file problems, make a backup first. Please do not post passwords, personal data or private save files publicly. If a report contains spoilers, say so before including details.

Please check existing reports before submitting a new one.

### A Note About Spoilers

Floppy//144 is a mystery game, and discovery is an important part of the experience.

Please avoid posting major revelations, puzzle solutions or unexpected discoveries in public issue titles.

If your report contains spoilers, clearly mark it as such.

**Let the next investigator discover the paperwork for themselves.**
